## Manual Calculation Methods (Formulas)

While the interactive digital arena handles live tracking effortlessly, understanding the core formulas running in the background is critical for realizing why wild guessing destroys your score.

### The True Net WPM Formula

The global standard for professional typing speed enforces the "5 Characters = 1 Word" rule and heavily penalizes uncorrected mistakes.

*   **Gross WPM** = (Total Keystrokes / 5) / Time (in minutes)
*   **Net WPM** = Gross WPM - (Uncorrected Errors / Time in minutes)

**Example Equation:** You take a 2-minute test. You type 800 total characters. You leave 6 errors uncorrected.
*   **Step 1 (Gross Words):** 800 characters / 5 = 160 Words. 
*   **Step 2 (Gross WPM):** 160 Words / 2 Minutes = 80 Gross WPM.
*   **Step 3 (Error Penalty):** 6 Errors / 2 Minutes = 3 Errors per minute penalty.
*   **Step 4 (Net WPM):** 80 Gross WPM - 3 Penalty.
*   **Final Answer:** Your final, officially verified score is exactly **77 Net WPM**.

*(Notice the brutal reality: The penalty is calculated per minute. If you type incredibly fast but leave massive amounts of red errors on the screen, your Net WPM will crash).*
