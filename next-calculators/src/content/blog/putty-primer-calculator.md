## Common Mistakes Users Make

When contractors and homeowners attempt to navigate the complex transition from raw plaster to flawless paint, they frequently fall into these catastrophic financial and physical traps:

1.  **Only Applying One Coat of Putty:** A single coat of putty will NEVER provide a mirror finish. The first coat shrinks as it dries into the deep pores of the plaster, leaving the wall slightly wavy. You must always calculate and apply a second coat to fill the minor imperfections.
2.  **Skipping the Sanding Process:** The calculator gives you the materials, but the physical labor is where the magic happens. After the second coat of putty dries, it MUST be aggressively sanded with fine grit sandpaper (e.g., 220 grit) until it feels like glass. If you apply primer over unsanded putty, the wall will look terrible.
3.  **Diluting Primer with Too Much Water:** Contractors often try to save money by watering down the primer so it covers more square footage. If you over-dilute primer, it loses its chemical ability to seal the putty. The top-coat paint will soak through, destroying the final finish. Always follow the manufacturer's exact dilution ratio.
4.  **Buying Interior Putty for Exterior Walls:** Interior acrylic putty will physically wash away if exposed to heavy rain. You must always use White Cement-based putty for exterior walls. Ensure your material order reflects the correct chemical type for the environment.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**1. What is the difference between Wall Putty and Primer?**
Wall Putty is a thick, cement or acrylic-based paste used to fill deep holes and physically level the rough plaster to a smooth finish. Primer is a thin, liquid resin applied *over* the dried putty to seal it, preventing the expensive top-coat paint from being absorbed into the wall.

**2. Can I apply paint directly over Wall Putty without Primer?**
Absolutely not. Dry wall putty is highly porous (like a sponge). If you paint directly over it, the putty will suck all the moisture out of the paint. You will end up using 3 times more paint to get a decent color, and the finish will be dull and powdery. Primer is a mandatory sealing layer.

**3. Do I really need 2 coats of Wall Putty?**
Yes, on fresh plaster. The first coat acts as a heavy filler to aggressively level the abrasive sand particles in the cement. The second coat acts as a fine finishing layer to achieve the glass-like smoothness required for luxury emulsion paints.

**4. How long should Putty dry before applying Primer?**
Wall putty must be absolutely, 100% dry before sanding and priming. Depending on humidity and temperature, you should generally wait a minimum of 6 to 12 hours between putty coats, and at least 24 hours before applying primer.

**5. How much area does 1kg of wall putty cover?**
For a standard double-coat application on fresh, rough plaster, 1kg of high-quality wall putty will cover roughly 10 to 15 square feet.

**6. How much area does 1 Liter of primer cover?**
Because primer is a thin liquid applied over an already smooth, puttied surface, it spreads very far. A single liter of standard interior water-based primer will cover roughly 100 to 140 square feet.

**7. Can I use Exterior Primer inside my house?**
You can, but it is a massive waste of money. Exterior primers are heavily engineered to resist UV radiation, algae, and severe monsoon rain, making them significantly more expensive. Interior primers are much cheaper and perfectly optimized for indoor use.
